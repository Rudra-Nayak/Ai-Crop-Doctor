# Fertilizer Guide — Agricultural Knowledge Base

## Nutrient Deficiency Identification and Treatment

### Nitrogen (N) Deficiency

#### Symptoms
- Uniform yellowing (chlorosis) starting from older/lower leaves
- Stunted growth and thin stems
- Reduced tillering in cereals
- Premature leaf senescence
- Poor fruit development

#### Affected Crops
All crops, especially cereals (rice, wheat, maize), leafy vegetables

#### Recommended Fertilizers
- Urea (46% N): 50-100 kg/ha based on crop requirement
- Ammonium sulfate (21% N): Preferred for paddy and acid-loving crops
- Calcium ammonium nitrate (25% N): For quick correction
- Organic: Well-decomposed farmyard manure (10-15 t/ha), vermicompost (5 t/ha)

#### Application Tips
- Split applications: 50% basal + 25% at tillering + 25% at flowering
- Apply during active growth stages
- Avoid excess — causes lodging, disease susceptibility, and environmental pollution

---

### Phosphorus (P) Deficiency

#### Symptoms
- Dark green to purplish-red discoloration of older leaves
- Stunted root development
- Delayed maturity and flowering
- Poor seed/fruit development
- Leaves may appear bluish-green

#### Affected Crops
All crops, critical for legumes, root vegetables, cereals

#### Recommended Fertilizers
- Single superphosphate (SSP, 16% P₂O₅): 125-250 kg/ha
- Diammonium phosphate (DAP, 46% P₂O₅): 50-100 kg/ha
- Rock phosphate: For acidic soils (long-term)
- Organic: Bone meal (20-25% P₂O₅), phospho-compost

#### Application Tips
- Apply as basal dose — phosphorus is immobile in soil
- Band placement near root zone is more efficient than broadcasting
- Acidic soils: Use rock phosphate or acidulate before application
- Alkaline soils: Apply with organic matter to improve availability

---

### Potassium (K) Deficiency

#### Symptoms
- Scorching or browning of leaf margins and tips, starting from older leaves
- Weak stems prone to lodging
- Increased susceptibility to diseases
- Poor fruit quality (small, thin-skinned)
- Reduced drought tolerance

#### Affected Crops
Potatoes, bananas, coconut, sugarcane, vegetables

#### Recommended Fertilizers
- Muriate of potash (MOP, 60% K₂O): 50-150 kg/ha
- Sulfate of potash (SOP, 50% K₂O): For chloride-sensitive crops (tobacco, grapes, potato)
- Organic: Wood ash (5-10% K₂O), banana pseudostem compost

#### Application Tips
- Split application: 50% basal + 50% at active growth
- Sandy soils require more frequent applications
- Avoid excess — can cause magnesium deficiency

---

### Zinc (Zn) Deficiency

#### Symptoms
- Interveinal chlorosis on young leaves
- Small, narrow leaves ("little leaf" syndrome)
- Shortened internodes giving a rosette appearance
- White or light tan patches on leaves (rice: "khaira disease")
- Poor grain filling

#### Affected Crops
Rice (very common), wheat, maize, citrus, mango

#### Recommended Fertilizers
- Zinc sulfate heptahydrate (21% Zn): 25-50 kg/ha soil application
- Zinc sulfate monohydrate (33% Zn): 15-25 kg/ha
- Foliar spray: ZnSO₄ 0.5% solution (5 g/L)
- Chelated zinc (Zn-EDTA): 5 kg/ha — more efficient but costly

#### Application Tips
- Soil application at sowing/transplanting
- Foliar spray for quick correction during growing season
- Alkaline and calcareous soils have high deficiency risk
- Apply with organic matter to improve availability

---

### Iron (Fe) Deficiency

#### Symptoms
- Interveinal chlorosis on youngest leaves (veins remain green)
- Severe cases: entire leaf turns white or pale yellow
- Common in alkaline and calcareous soils
- Known as "lime-induced chlorosis"

#### Affected Crops
Rice (direct-seeded), groundnut, soybean, sorghum, fruit trees

#### Recommended Fertilizers
- Ferrous sulfate (FeSO₄, 19% Fe): Soil application 25-50 kg/ha or foliar spray 1%
- Iron chelates (Fe-EDDHA): 5-10 kg/ha — most effective in alkaline soils
- Foliar spray: FeSO₄ 0.5% + citric acid 0.1% + urea 0.5%

#### Application Tips
- Soil applications often ineffective in high pH soils — prefer foliar
- Apply organic matter to reduce soil pH locally
- Green manuring helps improve iron availability
- Avoid excessive phosphorus and lime application

---

### Manganese (Mn) Deficiency

#### Symptoms
- Interveinal chlorosis on young leaves (similar to iron but less distinct)
- Gray or tan specks on oat and wheat leaves ("gray speck")
- Reduced seed viability
- "Marsh spot" in peas — brown internal discoloration of seeds

#### Affected Crops
Oats, wheat, peas, soybeans, citrus

#### Recommended Fertilizers
- Manganese sulfate (MnSO₄): Soil application 20-25 kg/ha
- Foliar spray: MnSO₄ 0.5% (5 g/L)
- Chelated manganese (Mn-EDTA): 2-5 kg/ha

#### Application Tips
- Foliar application is more effective than soil application
- Common in over-limed or alkaline soils
- Waterlogged soils can cause manganese toxicity — drain properly

## Crop-Specific Fertilizer Recommendations

### Rice (Irrigated Lowland)
| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Timing |
|----------|--------------------------|--------|
| Nitrogen | 120-150 | 50% basal, 25% tillering, 25% panicle initiation |
| Phosphorus | 60-80 (P₂O₅) | 100% basal |
| Potassium | 60-80 (K₂O) | 50% basal, 50% panicle initiation |
| Zinc | 25 (ZnSO₄) | Basal |

### Wheat
| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Timing |
|----------|--------------------------|--------|
| Nitrogen | 120-150 | 50% basal, 25% first irrigation, 25% second irrigation |
| Phosphorus | 60 (P₂O₅) | 100% basal |
| Potassium | 40-60 (K₂O) | 100% basal |

### Tomato
| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Timing |
|----------|--------------------------|--------|
| Nitrogen | 100-120 | 50% basal, 25% at 30 days, 25% at 60 days |
| Phosphorus | 60-80 (P₂O₅) | 100% basal |
| Potassium | 80-100 (K₂O) | 50% basal, 50% at fruiting |
| Calcium | 20-30 (CaO) | To prevent blossom end rot |

### Potato
| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Timing |
|----------|--------------------------|--------|
| Nitrogen | 150-200 | 50% basal, 50% at earthing up |
| Phosphorus | 100-150 (P₂O₅) | 100% basal |
| Potassium | 150 (K₂O) | 50% basal, 50% at earthing up |

### Maize
| Nutrient | Recommended Dose (kg/ha) | Timing |
|----------|--------------------------|--------|
| Nitrogen | 120-150 | 33% basal, 33% knee-height, 33% tasseling |
| Phosphorus | 60-80 (P₂O₅) | 100% basal |
| Potassium | 40-60 (K₂O) | 100% basal |
| Zinc | 25 (ZnSO₄) | Basal |
